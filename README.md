A comprehensive full-stack internal management platform designed to streamline business operations. This application provides complete content management, course administration, recruitment processing, and analytics in a single, cohesive dashboard.

**Live Demo:** [🔗 forwardsols.com](https://forwardsols.com)  
**Source Code:** [🔗 GitHub Repository](https://github.com/hey-Zayn/LCU)

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Admin Dashboard
- ** Comprehensive Overview:** Real-time analytics and performance metrics
- ** User Management:** Complete user role and permission system
- ** Content Analytics:** Form submissions and user engagement tracking

### 📝 Content Management
- ** Blog System:** Full CRUD operations for articles and publications
- ** Media Handling:** Image upload and management with Cloudinary
- ** SEO Optimization:** Meta tags and SEO-friendly URL structures

### 🎓 Course Management (LMS)
- ** Course Creation:** Build and organize learning materials
- ** Student Enrollment:** Manage user registrations and progress tracking
- ** Progress Analytics:** Monitor student engagement and completion rates

### 💼 Recruitment System
- ** Job Postings:** Create and manage job listings
- ** Applicant Tracking:** Full pipeline management from application to hire
- ** Automated Communications:** One-click status updates and notifications

### 📧 Automated Notifications
- ** Email Campaigns:** Nodemailer integration for automated communications
- ** Real-time Alerts:** Instant notifications for form submissions
- ** Applicant Updates:** Automated emails for interview invitations and rejections

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### Frontend
- ** Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- ** Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Shadcn/UI
- ** Animations:** GSAP, Framer Motion
- ** 3D Graphics:** Three.js, Spline
- ** State Management:** Zustand

### Backend
- ** Runtime:** Node.js
- ** Framework:** Express.js
- ** Database:** MongoDB
- ** Authentication:** JWT
- ** Email Service:** Nodemailer
- ** File Storage:** Cloudinary

### Deployment
- ** Frontend:** Vercel
- ** Backend:** Railway/Render
- ** Database:** MongoDB Atlas
